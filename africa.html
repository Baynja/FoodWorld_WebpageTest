Meet the local favorites

Nigeria
SIGNATURE FOOD · NIGERIA

Jollof Rice

Tomato-rich rice cooked with peppers and warm spices.

THE STORY

Jollof has roots in West African food history and developed into numerous national variations.

WHY IT'S LOVED

It is central to parties and friendly debates about which version tastes best.

02 / 05
Ethiopia
SIGNATURE FOOD · ETHIOPIA

Injera with Doro Wat

Tangy flatbread paired with a richly spiced chicken stew.

THE STORY

Injera and communal platters have long been important to Ethiopian dining traditions.

WHY IT'S LOVED

The shared eating style and distinctive fermented teff flatbread make the meal memorable.

03 / 05
Morocco
SIGNATURE FOOD · MOROCCO

Couscous

Steamed semolina grains served with vegetables and savory broth.

THE STORY

Couscous is a long-established North African staple closely connected with Amazigh food heritage.

WHY IT'S LOVED

It is a popular family meal and a symbol of hospitality.

04 / 05
Egypt
SIGNATURE FOOD · EGYPT

Koshari

A hearty street-food bowl of rice, lentils, pasta, and tomato sauce.

THE STORY

Koshari developed in modern Egypt through the blending of diverse staple ingredients.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Affordable, filling, and easy to customize, it is a cherished everyday dish.

05 / 05
South Africa
SIGNATURE FOOD · SOUTH AFRICA

Bobotie

Spiced baked minced meat topped with a savory egg custard.

THE STORY

Bobotie reflects layered influences in South African, especially Cape Malay, cooking.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Its balance of sweet and savory flavors makes it a recognizable home-style dish.
