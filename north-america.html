United States
SIGNATURE FOOD · UNITED STATES

Hamburger

A grilled patty served inside a bun with toppings.

THE STORY

The modern hamburger rose to prominence in the United States around the turn of the twentieth century.

WHY IT'S LOVED

It became a worldwide fast-food symbol with countless regional variations.

02 / 05
Mexico
SIGNATURE FOOD · MEXICO

Tacos

Soft tortillas filled with meats, vegetables, and fresh salsa.

THE STORY

Taco traditions grew from Mexican corn-based foodways and regional cooking cultures.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Their versatility and lively street-food culture make them famous around the world.

03 / 05
Canada
SIGNATURE FOOD · CANADA

Poutine

French fries covered with cheese curds and savory gravy.

THE STORY

Poutine originated in Quebec in the mid-twentieth century.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Its indulgent combination has grown into a recognizable Canadian comfort food.

04 / 05
Jamaica
SIGNATURE FOOD · JAMAICA

Jerk Chicken

Smoky, spicy chicken seasoned with jerk herbs and peppers.

THE STORY

Jerk cooking draws on Jamaican culinary traditions, including Maroon and Indigenous influences.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Its distinctive heat, smoke, and spice have earned international recognition.

05 / 05
Cuba
SIGNATURE FOOD · CUBA

Ropa Vieja

Shredded beef simmered in tomato-pepper sauce.

THE STORY

Ropa vieja is associated with Cuban home cooking and has earlier Spanish connections.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Its deeply flavored slow-cooked texture makes it a beloved traditional meal.
