Meet the local favorites

01 / 05
Italy
SIGNATURE FOOD · ITALY

Neapolitan Pizza

A thin-centered pizza with puffed crust, tomato, and mozzarella.

THE STORY

Pizza’s modern Neapolitan style developed in Naples and spread globally.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Its few quality ingredients and quick, high-heat baking have made it internationally famous.

02 / 05
France
SIGNATURE FOOD · FRANCE

Croissant

A buttery, flaky pastry with a golden crust.

THE STORY

The modern croissant emerged from French viennoiserie traditions inspired by Central European pastries.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Its delicate layers and café-breakfast association make it a culinary symbol.

03 / 05
Spain
SIGNATURE FOOD · SPAIN

Paella

Saffron-scented rice cooked in a broad shallow pan.

THE STORY

Paella originated around Valencia and developed many regional versions.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Its communal serving style and flavorful rice crust have made it celebrated worldwide.

04 / 05
Germany
SIGNATURE FOOD · GERMANY

Bratwurst

Grilled seasoned sausage, often served with bread and mustard.

THE STORY

Many regions of Germany have their own bratwurst traditions dating back centuries.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Its place in festivals, markets, and casual meals makes it especially well known.

05 / 05
United Kingdom
SIGNATURE FOOD · UNITED KINGDOM

Fish and Chips

Crispy battered fish with thick-cut fried potatoes.

THE STORY

Fish-and-chip shops became established in Britain during the nineteenth century.

WHY IT'S LOVED

The dish is strongly associated with British takeaway and seaside food culture.
