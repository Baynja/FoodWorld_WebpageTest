About FoodWorld.

A student-created guide to the dishes that connect people across borders.

OUR PURPOSE

Discover cultures through food.

FoodWorld is a student website project designed to introduce visitors to food traditions from around the globe. We believe food is one of the easiest and most enjoyable ways to learn about different cultures.

We organize the world into six regions, featuring five selected countries in each region and one signature dish for every country. Explore the dishes, read their stories, and learn how to cook a simplified version at home.

Our featured dishes are curated examples, not a definitive ranking of the “most famous” foods. Regional traditions vary, and every country has many wonderful dishes worth discovering.
