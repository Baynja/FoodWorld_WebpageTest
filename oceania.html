Flavors of Australia & Oceania

Island ingredients, earth-oven feasts, and comforting Pacific favorites.

5 MUST-KNOW DISHES

Meet the local favorites

Australia · New Zealand · Fiji · Samoa · Papua New Guinea

01 / 05
Australia
SIGNATURE FOOD · AUSTRALIA

Meat Pie

A handheld pastry pie filled with rich minced-meat gravy.

THE STORY

Meat pies have been part of Australian bakery and sporting-event culture for generations.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Their convenience and familiar savory filling make them a popular classic.

02 / 05
New Zealand
SIGNATURE FOOD · NEW ZEALAND

Hāngī

A traditional Māori method of cooking food slowly in an earth oven.

THE STORY

Hāngī is an important Māori cooking tradition often associated with gatherings and celebrations.

WHY IT'S LOVED

It is renowned for its communal preparation and distinctive earthy cooking method.

03 / 05
Fiji
SIGNATURE FOOD · FIJI

Kokoda

Fish marinated in citrus and served with coconut cream.

THE STORY

Kokoda is a well-known Fijian seafood preparation that showcases Pacific ingredients.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Citrus, coconut, and fresh vegetables give it a distinctive island flavor.

04 / 05
Samoa
SIGNATURE FOOD · SAMOA

Palusami

Leaf-wrapped coconut cream, traditionally baked in an earth oven.

THE STORY

Palusami reflects Samoan traditions of cooking with taro leaves and coconut.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Its creamy texture and leaf-wrapped presentation make it memorable.

05 / 05
Papua New Guinea
SIGNATURE FOOD · PAPUA NEW GUINEA

Mumu

A communal earth-oven meal of vegetables and meat.

THE STORY

Mumu is a long-standing Papua New Guinean cooking method used at family and community events.

WHY IT'S LOVED

The shared preparation connects food with celebration and community.
