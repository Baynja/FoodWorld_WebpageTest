5 MUST-KNOW DISHES

Meet the local favorites

01 / 05
Myanmar
SIGNATURE FOOD · MYANMAR

Mohinga

A fragrant fish-and-rice-noodle soup, often enjoyed for breakfast.

THE STORY

Mohinga is deeply woven into everyday Burmese food culture and is sold in markets and teashops.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Its comforting broth, fresh toppings, and presence across the country make it a beloved national favorite.

02 / 05
Thailand
SIGNATURE FOOD · THAILAND

Pad Thai

Stir-fried rice noodles with tamarind, egg, and fresh garnishes.

THE STORY

Pad Thai became widely promoted in Thailand during the twentieth century and developed into a popular street-food dish.

WHY IT'S LOVED

The sweet, sour, salty balance and fast wok cooking have made it internationally recognizable.

03 / 05
China
SIGNATURE FOOD · CHINA

Peking Duck

Crisp-skinned roasted duck served with thin pancakes and sauce.

THE STORY

Peking duck is associated with Beijing and has roots in imperial-era Chinese cooking.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Its elaborate roasting technique and celebratory presentation make it iconic.

04 / 05
Singapore
SIGNATURE FOOD · SINGAPORE

Hainanese Chicken Rice

Tender poached chicken with seasoned rice and chili sauce.

THE STORY

Adapted from Hainanese cooking traditions, the dish became a signature of Singapore’s hawker culture.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Its simple ingredients, distinctive rice, and flavorful sauces are celebrated across the city.

05 / 05
India
SIGNATURE FOOD · INDIA

Biryani

Fragrant layered rice with spices, herbs, and meat or vegetables.

THE STORY

Biryani evolved through several regional traditions influenced by Persianate and South Asian cooking.

WHY IT'S LOVED

Its rich fragrance, celebratory role, and many regional varieties make it widely loved.
